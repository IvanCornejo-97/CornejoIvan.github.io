Projects

Three working Excel workbooks. Each runs on live formulas, uses sample data and opens with a "How It Works" tab explaining every sheet.

Project 1

License & Certification Tracker

Required licenses and certifications for 40 employees at an agricultural and packing company. It counts down to every expiration and tells HR exactly who needs a renewal reminder.

  1. Compliance KPIsLicenses tracked, current, expiring within 60 days, expired and the compliance rate.
  2. Status by license typeCDL, DOT medical card, HACCP, food handler, CPR and pesticide applicator, each split by status, with a chart.
  3. Action by departmentHow many licenses need action in each department and how many renewals are already submitted.

Key formulas

=IF(H5<0,"Expired",IF(H5<=30,"Expires <30 days",IF(H5<=$P$2,"Expires <60 days","Current")))Status from days left and an adjustable warning window
=IFERROR(INDEX(names,MATCH(A5,ids,0)),"Check ID")Pulls each employee's name and department
=COUNTIF(days_left,">=0")/totalCompliance rate

Project 2

Payroll Timesheet Calculator

Daily clock-in and clock-out punches for 12 employees, turned into a weekly payroll summary. It applies California overtime rules and calculates gross pay for each person.

  1. Week selectorChoose the pay week. The overtime threshold is an input, not a hard-coded number.
  2. California overtimeOver 8 hours a day pays 1.5x and over 12 pays 2x. Regular hours past 40 a week also pay 1.5x, with no double counting.
  3. Hours chartRegular, overtime and double-time hours stacked by employee.

Key formulas

=MAX(0,MIN(G5,$O$2)-$O$1)Daily overtime: hours between 8 and 12
=INDEX(rate,MATCH(A6,id,0))Looks up each employee's hourly rate
=C5-WEEKDAY(C5,3)Finds the Monday of each pay week

Project 3

Appointment & Scheduling Tracker

A month of bookings for a service business, about 350 appointments. It catches double-bookings automatically and reports no-shows, reschedules and how many hours each person is booked.

  1. Booking KPIsAppointments, completed, no-show rate, reschedules, Spanish-speaking customers and overlaps to fix.
  2. Staff workloadAppointments, hours booked, completions, no-shows and average appointments per day for each person.
  3. Hours chartHours booked by staff member, so uneven schedules are easy to rebalance.

Key formulas

=IF(COUNTIFS(staff,J5,date,B5,start,"<"&F5,end,">"&D5)>1,"Overlap","OK")Flags any booking that overlaps another for the same person
=D5+E5/1440End time from start time and minutes
=SUMPRODUCT(1/COUNTIF(dates,dates))Counts unique working days
