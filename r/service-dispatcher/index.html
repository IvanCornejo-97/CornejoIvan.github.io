Projects

Three working Excel workbooks. Each runs on live formulas, uses sample data and opens with a "How It Works" tab explaining every sheet.

Project 1

Appointment & Scheduling Tracker

A month of bookings for a service business, about 350 appointments. It catches double-bookings automatically and reports no-shows, reschedules and how many hours each person is booked.

  1. Booking KPIsAppointments, completed, no-show rate, reschedules, Spanish-speaking customers and overlaps to fix.
  2. Staff workloadAppointments, hours booked, completions, no-shows and average appointments per day for each person.
  3. Hours chartHours booked by staff member, so uneven schedules are easy to rebalance.

Key formulas

=IF(COUNTIFS(staff,J5,date,B5,start,"<"&F5,end,">"&D5)>1,"Overlap","OK")Flags any booking that overlaps another for the same person
=D5+E5/1440End time from start time and minutes
=SUMPRODUCT(1/COUNTIF(dates,dates))Counts unique working days

Project 2

Dispatch Route Tracker

A month of delivery routes, about 620 of them, turned into a dispatch dashboard. It shows packages moved, stop completion, on-time returns, rescues and how every driver performed.

  1. Headline KPIsTotal routes, packages, stop completion, on-time returns, rescues and average hours per route.
  2. Day-by-day tableRoutes, packages, on-time % and rescues per day, built with COUNTIF, SUMIF and COUNTIFS.
  3. Trend chartsDaily package volume and on-time return rate, so bad days stand out at a glance.

Key formulas

=(I5-H5)*24Hours on route (Excel stores time as a fraction of a day)
=IF(I5<=$Q$1,"Yes","No")On-time check against an adjustable cutoff
=COUNTIFS(driver,A5,on_time,"Yes")/B5On-time rate per driver

Project 3

Invoice & Accounts Receivable Tracker

An invoice register for a small appliance sales and repair business. It works out every balance and due date, flags overdue accounts and builds an aging report on its own.

  1. Collections at a glanceTotal invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue and the collection rate.
  2. Aging bucketsUnpaid balances sorted into Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days, with a chart.
  3. Balances by customerCustomers with overdue balances are highlighted for follow-up calls.

Key formulas

=IF(G5>0,MAX(0,$N$1-H5),0)Days past due, unpaid invoices only
=SUMIF(bucket,A9,balance)Total balance in each aging bucket
=SUMIFS(amount,date,">="&A5,date,"<="&B5)Monthly totals with EOMONTH
